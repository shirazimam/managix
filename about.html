About Managix

Ethical, practical technology consulting for organizations that want better systems.

Managix Technology and Management Services LLP helps organizations integrate information technology into daily operations through consulting, development, deployment and support.

Who we are

Driven by complete IT solutions.

Managix is an IT consulting, development and service providing company. We help organizations and institutions integrate technology into their system of operation.

Our work covers planning, deploying and maintaining turnkey systems and projects. We address diverse customer needs through a full-service portfolio and aim to provide cost-effective solutions backed by IT knowledge, business understanding and practical execution.

The company philosophy is based on ethical business and socially responsible practices. We focus on high standards of business conduct, customer satisfaction and stakeholder trust.

Vision and mission

Simple, effective and ethical IT solutions.

Vision

To provide the most effective, simple and efficient IT solutions to businesses and help build an interface of trust and security in the online world.

Mission

To provide ethical, effective and viable IT solutions that ease the functionality and operational structure of organizations through expertise and experience.
